Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2021-06-18
Cyanobacteria is not currently detectable by satellite in western Lake Erie. So far, only traces of cyanobacteria are
present in western Lake Erie. The seasonal forecast will be issued on June 30, check Ohio Sea Grant for information.
Cyanobacteria are now detectable in Sandusky Bay, which has an annual bloom that is unrelated to western Lake Erie
blooms. No recent toxin data currently available.
The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for
technical Questions. Last Updated: 2021-06-18 11 PM EDT --Stumpf
