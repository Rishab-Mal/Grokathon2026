Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
2024-10-10
 Summary
 The cyanobacteria bloom is present in western Lake Erie but cloud cover or winds above 9.0 mph prevent determining the
area. The Microcystis bloom is present in western Lake Erie, but at reduced concentrations as compared to mid-September,
likely due to wind-driven water column mixing and sediment resuspension. As of October 8th, small patches of high-biomass
bloom are observed from Monroe, MI to Maumee Bay, OH and along the Ohio coast from Cedar Point National Wildlife Refuge
to Port Clinton. Sandusky Bay continues to have a local bloom of mixed cyanobacteria. Toxins have been detected below the
recreational limit. They can be highly concentrated in scums! If you see scum, keep your pets and yourself out of the water. In
the satellite imagery or bloom forecast position products, any areas that are orange or red are likely to have scum, especially
during calm winds, see Mixing Forecast product. --NCCOS HAB Forecasting Team 8 October 2024.
The past few days of imagery can be seen at the HAB monitoring site. The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the National
Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for technical Questions. Last Updated: 2024-10-10 01 PM EST
