Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Bulletin
19 September, 2019, Bulletin 24
Analysis
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom continues to decline in the western basin of Lake Erie. Observed winds (9/16-18) this week caused
mixing, further reducing surface concentrations. In recent satellite imagery (9/18), Microcystis concentrations remain detectable in
Maumee Bay, in patches along the Michigan and Ohio coastlines, and along Pelee Island. Measured toxin concentrations are below the
detection limit in most of the sampling locations, and below the recreational threshold throughout the bloom extent. The persistent
cyanobacteria bloom in Sandusky Bay continues.
Forecasts
Winds (5-16 kn) forecast today through Sunday (9/19-22) will promote mixing and northeastward transport of remaining surface
Microcystis concentrations. -Davis, Keeney
