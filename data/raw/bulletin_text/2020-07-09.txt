Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/07/10 09:26 EDT
Cyanobacteria is present in the western basin of Lake Erie. Satellite imagery indicates detectable, but relatively low, 
Microcystis concentrations in Maumee Bay, extending up to 10 miles north along the Michigan coast. Sampling from
7/6 indicates toxin concentrations have increased since last week, but continue to remain below the recreational
threshold.
Forecast winds (5-11kn) through Monday (7/13) will promote northward transport and mixing of Microcystis
concentrations. - Davis, Keeney
