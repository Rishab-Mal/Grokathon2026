Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/09/11 08:41 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery has been
heavily clouded, limiting analysis. Modeled outputs predict the bloom to be localized in the Maumee Bay area and
offshore the Ohio coast in the western basin. Periods of strong winds likely mixed surface bloom concentrations.
Sampling was delayed by the federal holiday, though previous samples (9/1) indicated toxin concentrations are below
the recreational threshold at all locations. Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (7-18 kn) will promote additional periods of mixing and minimize transport of Microcystis
concentrations through Monday (9/14) . -Keeney, Davis
