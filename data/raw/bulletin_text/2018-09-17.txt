Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Bulletin
17 September, 2018, Bulletin 26
Analysis
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery (9/16) shows the bloom
extending from the Maumee Bay east along the coast of Ohio to the Magee Marsh Wildlife Area. A large sediment plume throughout the
western basin may be obscuring detected concentrations. Winds observed over the weekend (9/14-16) likely promoted slight mixing of
Microcystis concentrations. Toxin concentrations were detected at all sampling sites but remain below the recreational threshold. Keep
pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming. The persistent cyanobacteria bloom in Sandusky Bay continues.
Forecasts
Winds (3-20 kn) forecast today through Thursday (9/17-20) will promote mixing of surface waters and westward transport of Microcystis
concentrations. --Lalime, Ludema
