Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Bulletin
06 September, 2018, Bulletin 23
Analysis
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery (9/5) shows the bloom
extending north along the Michigan coast to Point Mouillee State Game Area, east along the Ohio coast to the Toussaint River, and
through the Bass Islands extending up to 17 miles east of Pelee Island.  Calm winds observed this week (9/3-5) may have promoted scum
formation of Microcystis concentrations. Toxin concentrations were detected at all sampling sites but remain below the recreational
threshold. Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming. The persistent cyanobacteria bloom in Sandusky Bay
continues.
Forecasts
Winds (10-25 kn) forecast today through Monday (9/6-10) will promote mixing of surface waters and the southward transport of
Microcystis concentrations today through Sunday (9/6-9). --Davis, Lalime
