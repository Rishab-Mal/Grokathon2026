Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/08/12 09:11 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Over the weekend, dense chlorophyll
was observed at the surface, followed by periods of mixing and net eastern transport. The bloom is present in Maumee
Bay, extending north along the Michigan coast to Stony Point, along the Ohio coast to Lacarpe Creek, and into the
western basin up to 6 miles northeast of Isle St. George. Sampling (8/4) indicates toxin concentrations remain below the
recreational threshold at all locations. Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (5-11 kn) through Thursday (8/13) are favorable for mixing and minimal transport of Microcystis
concentrations within the western basin. -Keeney, Davis
