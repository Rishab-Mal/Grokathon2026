Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Bulletin
23 September, 2019, Bulletin 25
Analysis
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom in the western basin of Lake Erie continues to decline. Conditions observed since last week (9/19-
9/22) allowed surface chlorophyll to increase (9/21), but was followed by mixing (9/22). Recent satellite imagery (9/21), shows the bloom
is present just offshore the Michigan coast from Maumee bay to William C. Sterling State Park; and along the Ohio coast 3-5 miles
offshore from Magee Marsh Wildlife Area. Microcystis concentrations are below detection in most areas, but remain detectable in
Maumee Bay. Measured toxin concentrations are below the recreational threshold throughout the bloom extent. The persistent
cyanobacteria bloom in Sandusky Bay continues.
Forecasts
Winds (5-25 kn) forecast today through Thursday (9/23-25) will promote mixing and net eastern transport of remaining surface
Microcystis concentrations. -Keeney, Jima
