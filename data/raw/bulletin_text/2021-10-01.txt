Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2021-10-01
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom in western Lake Erie has an approximate area of 180 square miles, which is an
increase in area since Sep 29. The bloom is present from east of Maumee Bay to Stony Point north of Monroe, MI. The
plume of brown water from the Maumee River (from rains last week) is present but dissipating in Maumee Bay. The
water around the Islands is still somewhat turbid from sediment churned up by strong winds over the last week. Toxins
have been detected below the recreational limit. They can be highly concentrated in scums! If you see scum, keep your
pets and yourself out of the water. In the satellite imagery or bloom forecast position products, any areas that are
orange or red are likely to have scum, especially during calm winds, see Mixing Forecast product. --Stumpf 09/30
The past few days of imagery can be seen at the HAB monitoring site. The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the
National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for technical Questions. Last Updated:
