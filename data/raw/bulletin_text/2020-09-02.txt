Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/09/03 09:52 EDT
**Due to the upcoming federal holiday, the next forecast analysis will be issued on Tuesday, September 8. .
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery indicates the
bloom is present along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, along the Ohio coast to towards the Marblehead Peninsula, and
offshore up to 8 miles from West Sister Island. Recent mixing of western basin bloom concentrations has continued to
reduce visible surface chlorophyll, no longer visible through the islands towards Point Pelee. Sampling (9/1) indicates
toxin concentrations continue to decrease and are below the recreational threshold at all locations. Keep pets and
yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
