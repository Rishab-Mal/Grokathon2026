Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2021-08-01
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom in western Lake Erie has an approximate area of 40 square miles, which is
unchanged from Jul 28. The bloom is patchy, including some areas of scum in calm water, along and near the Ohio
coast west of Catawba Island almost to Maumee Bay. Sandusky Bay has some traces of cyanobacteria. This is
unrelated to western Lake Erie blooms. We have not detected blooms elsewhere in the lake. The seasonal forecast
was issued June 30. Toxins have been detected below the recreational limit. They can be highly concentrated in
scums! If you see scum, keep your pets and yourself out of the water. In the satellite imagery or bloom forecast position
products, any areas that are orange or red are likely to have scum, especially during calm winds, see Mixing Forecast
product. --Stumpf 07/30
