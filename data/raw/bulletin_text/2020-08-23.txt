Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/08/24 09:48 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Satellite imagery indicates the bloom
is present along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, along the Ohio coast to Catawba Island, through the basin to Pelee
Island, and up to 7 miles southwest of Pelee Point. Eastern transport of bloom concentrations was observed over the
weekend, though mixing has reduced the amount detectable at the surface, particularly for regions around the islands
where chlorophyll is low. Scum is visible in the Maumee Bay area. Sampling (8/17) indicates toxin concentrations
exceed the recreational threshold in one location, though most remain below the recreational limit. Keep pets and
yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (5-19 kn) will promote scum formation in the Maumee Bay area through Tuesday (8/25), followed by
mixing and minimal transport of Microcystis concentrations (8/26-27). -Keeney, Davis
