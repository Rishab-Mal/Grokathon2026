Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/10/02 09:01 EDT
Microcystis cyanobacteria continues to decrease in concentration and extent. Recent satellite imagery shows small
patches of Microcystis remain visible in Maumee Bay and along- and offshore Brest Bay on the Michigan coast. Recent
sampling (9/28) indicates toxin concentrations continue to decrease and are below the recreational threshold at all
locations.
Water temperatures have now dropped below 65 °F (18 °C), and rapid decline of any remaining Microcystis is expected.
Forecast winds (3-18 kn) through Monday (10/5) will continue to promote mixing of any remaining Microcystis
concentrations. -Davis, Keeney
