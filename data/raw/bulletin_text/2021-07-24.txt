Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2021-07-24
Cyanobacteria is not currently detectable by satellite in western Lake Erie. Traces of cyanobacteria are detectable in
Sandusky Bay. This is unrelated to western Lake Erie blooms. The seasonal forecast was issued June 30. No recent
toxin data currently available. --Tomlinson 07/23
Lake Erie current Sentinel-3B satellite imagery from the Ocean and Land Color Imager (OLCI) on Jul 22, 2021,
showing bloom location and extent in the western basin. Grey indicates clouds or missing data. The estimated
threshold of cyanobacteria detection is 20,000 cells/mL. Inset shows truecolor image of the entire lake. Data derived
from Copernicus Sentinel-3.

Forecast surface bloom position for Jul 24, modeled from the last satellite image with water currents estimated from the
Lake Erie Operational Forecast System (LEOFS). Potential for bloom movement is forecast in 3-dimensions with a
hydrodynamic model using satellite imagery and currents. The modeled output does not contain clouds. Black indicates
the absence of chlorophyll and gray indicates area with no data. The arrows show forecasted currents. Water
temperature and winds (in magenta) are the averages for the western basin from the model.
