Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/09/17 12:37 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria is still present in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery has
been partially obscured by smoke from the western wildfires, limiting analysis at the coast. Imagery indicates the bloom
is present along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, along the Ohio coast to Magee Marsh Wildlife Area, and extending
offshore up to 8 miles east from Maumee Bay. No scums have been reported. Sampling (9/9) indicates toxin
concentrations are below the recreational threshold at all locations.
Water temperatures are approaching 68 °F (20 °C), when an increase in bloom concentrations will no longer be possible.
Forecast winds (8-20 kn) will promote further mixing and northward transport of Microcystis concentrations through
Monday (9/21). -Davis, Keeney
