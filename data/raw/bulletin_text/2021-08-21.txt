Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2021-08-21
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom in western Lake Erie has an approximate area of 360 square miles, which is an
increase in area since Aug 19. The bloom extends from Monroe, MI to Marblehead, OH and out to the islands. Near
Monroe the bloom has extended offshore into the middle of the lake. In areas with orange or red color, scum is likely
during calm winds. The seasonal forecast was issued June 30. No recent toxin data currently available. --Stumpf 08/21
The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for
technical Questions. Last Updated: 2021-08-21 11 PM EDT
