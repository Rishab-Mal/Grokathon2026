Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/09/01 09:22 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery indicates the
bloom is present along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, along the Ohio coast to the Marblehead Peninsula, and offshore
up to 5 miles from Point Pelee. Very low concentrations are visible extending into the central basin. Mixing of western
basin bloom concentrations was observed over the weekend, decreasing surface chlorophyll in the Maumee Bay region.
Sampling (8/24) indicates toxin concentrations are below the recreational threshold at all locations. Keep pets and
yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (5-18 kn) will promote scum formation on Tuesday (9/1) followed by mixing and minimized transport of
Microcystis concentrations through Thursday (9/3). -Davis, Keeney
