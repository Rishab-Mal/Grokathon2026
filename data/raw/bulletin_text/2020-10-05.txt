Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/10/06 09:39 EDT
Microcystis cyanobacteria continues to decrease in concentration and extent. Recent satellite imagery shows remaining 
Microcystis concentrations present along- and offshore the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, and spilling out of Maumee
Bay along the Ohio Coast to Metzger Marsh Wildlife Area. Recent samples (9/28) indicate toxin concentrations are
below the recreational threshold at all locations.
Water temperatures have dropped below 60 °F (15 °C), and rapid decline of any remaining Microcystis is expected.
Forecast winds (4-25 kn) through Thursday (10/8) will promote mixing of remaining Microcystis concentrations.
-Keeney, Davis
