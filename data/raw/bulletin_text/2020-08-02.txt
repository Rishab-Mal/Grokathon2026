Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/08/03 10:05 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. The bloom is present in Maumee
Bay, extending north along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay, along the Ohio coast towards Metzger Marsh Wildlife
Area, and offshore towards West Sister Island. Calm winds observed over the weekend promoted scum formation in
Maumee Bay and along the Michigan coast. Recent sampling (7/28) indicates toxin concentrations are below the
recreational threshold at all locations. Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (4-17 kn) through Thursday (8/6) will promote southward transport of Microcystis concentrations along
the Michigan coast and eastward transport along the Ohio coast. Winds are favorable for mixing of Microcystis
concentrations through Thursday. -Davis, Keeney
