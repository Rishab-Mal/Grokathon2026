Analysis
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery (9/7) is obscured by clouds
over most of the western basin, but shows concentrations in Maumee Bay.  Winds observed over the weekend (9/7-9) promoted mixing
of Microcystis concentrations. Toxin concentrations were detected at all sampling sites but remain below the recreational threshold.
Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming. The persistent cyanobacteria bloom in Sandusky Bay continues.
Forecasts
Winds (3-12 kn) forecast tonight through Thursday (9/10-13) will promote slight mixing of surface waters. Clouds will likely obscure the
lake through Thursday. --Ludema, Davis
