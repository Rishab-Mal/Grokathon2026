Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2023-09-21
The cyanobacteria bloom in western Lake Erie has an approximate area of 20 square miles, which is a decrease in
area since Sep 19. Clouds obscured recent satellite imagery. As of Sep 19, 2023, the cyanobacteria bloom extends
from Stony Point, MI to about Port Clinton, OH. The western basin bloom is a mix of Microcystis and Dolichospermum.
Sandusky Bay has a local bloom of mixed cyanobacteria. No recent toxin data currently available. --A. Meredith
09/21/2023.
The past few days of imagery can be seen at the HAB monitoring site. The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the
National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for technical Questions. Last Updated:
