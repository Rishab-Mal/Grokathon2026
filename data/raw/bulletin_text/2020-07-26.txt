Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/07/27 09:40 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria continues in the western basin of Lake Erie. The bloom is present in Maumee
Bay, extending north along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay and into the western basin approximately 3 miles northwest
of West Sister Island. Over the weekend, scums were observed near Maumee State Park and Luna Pier, followed by
periods of mixing. Recent sampling (7/20) indicates toxin concentrations remain below the recreational threshold at
most locations. Keep pets and yourself out of the water in areas where scum is forming.
Forecast winds (5-14 kn) through Thursday (7/30) will promote mixing and transport of Microcystis concentrations
north along the Michigan coast and east into the western basin towards the Islands. - Keeney, Davis
