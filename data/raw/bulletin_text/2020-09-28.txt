Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecasts - Last Updated 2020/09/29 11:17 EDT
A bloom of Microcystis cyanobacteria is still present in the western basin of Lake Erie. Recent satellite imagery shows
the bloom located along the Michigan coast to Brest Bay and up to 3 miles offshore. Bloom decline and mixing of the
remaining surface concentrations were observed over the weekend, decreasing Microcystis concentrations along- and
offshore the Ohio coast to no longer visible. Recent sampling (9/21) indicates toxin concentrations are below the
recreational threshold at all locations.
Water temperatures have dropped below 68 °F (20 °C), which should continue to decrease bloom concentrations
throughout the week. Forecast winds (5-22 kn) through Thursday (10/1) will continue to promote mixing while
minimizing transport of remaining Microcystis concentrations. -Davis, Keeney
