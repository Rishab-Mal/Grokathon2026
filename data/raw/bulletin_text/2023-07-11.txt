Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2023-07-11
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom in western Lake Erie has an approximate area of 100 square miles, which is an
increase in area since Jul 07. The cyanobacterial bloom extends from eastern Maumee Bay north to Monroe, MI, with
scum present during light winds. The Central basin appears to have a separate bloom at low concentration, which is
not unusual for early July (it usually disappears in mid-July). No toxin data currently available. --R.Stumpf 07/11/2023
The past few days of imagery can be seen at the HAB monitoring site. The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the
National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for technical Questions. Last Updated:
