Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast
Imagery Analysis & Forecast - 2022-09-30
The Microcystis cyanobacteria bloom is present in western Lake Erie but cloud cover and winds prevent determining
the area. The bloom extends from Stony Point, MI to Port Clinton, OH, with small patches extending offshore Catawba
Island. Sandusky Bay has a local bloom of cyanobacteria, which is persistent in most years.
No recent toxin data currently available. --S. Tomlinson 09/30
The past few days of imagery can be seen at the HAB monitoring site. The Lake Erie Forecast is operated by the
National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science. Contact hab@noaa.gov for technical Questions. Last Updated:
